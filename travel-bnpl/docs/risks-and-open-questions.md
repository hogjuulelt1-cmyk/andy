# Risks and open questions

Items marked **(verify)** are assumptions, not confirmed facts.

## Legal / regulatory

1. Taking money from Korean customers needs a Korean legal entity or a PG partner (Toss Payments, KG Inicis). Running our own BNPL in Korea falls under electronic finance rules (전자금융업); avoid in v1.
2. Even 0% installments could be treated as consumer credit if we carry the receivable ourselves **(verify with a lawyer)**. Using card 무이자 할부 via PG avoids this.
3. Selling packages needs a tour operator licence and insurance in Mongolia, and a Korean partner/agency setup **(verify)**.
4. Korean visa-free entry to Mongolia **(verify for 2026)**.
5. KYC/AML for cross-border settlement KRW → MNT.

## Operational

1. Working capital: camps and drivers want prepayment; summer prices spike.
2. Safety and quality: roads, vehicle breakdowns, accidents. One bad incident in Korean media can kill the brand. Need vehicle standards and travel insurance.
3. Shortage of Korean-speaking guides in summer.
4. Seasonality: Jun–Sep busy, rest empty. Build winter packages.
5. Summer ICN–ULN flights are expensive and sell out. Negotiate block seats with airlines.
6. Group fill risk: departures that don't reach minimum size.

## Competition

- Korean-owned tour companies already in Mongolia.
- Korean OTAs (MyRealTrip, Klook) sell Mongolia packages.
- Our edge: companion matching + trusted standard packages + small upfront payment.
