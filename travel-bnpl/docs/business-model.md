# Business model

## Decisions made by Anthony (2026-09-30)

- **Not layaway.** Trip is locked by the upfront payment, not after full payment.
- **Upfront payment covers the flight cost.**
- **Remainder in 0% installments.** No interest, no fees.
- **We sell our own tour packages** and profit from the package margin.
- **Target: young Koreans travelling to Mongolia** (inbound).

## Where money comes from

- Package margin 15–25% (we buy vehicle, driver, guide, ger camps at wholesale and set the retail price).
- Add-ons with high margin: Naadam tickets (2–3x price in July), photo shoots, domestic flights, eSIM, insurance.
- No interest revenue.

## Unit economics (example, estimates only)

Example in MNT from the original analysis; the shape holds in KRW:

|                                                | Amount                    |
| ---------------------------------------------- | ------------------------- |
| Package total                                  | 3,000,000 ₮               |
| Upfront (flight)                               | 1,200,000 ₮               |
| Installments                                   | 1,800,000 ₮ over 3 months |
| Margin 20%                                     | ~600,000 ₮                |
| Cost of capital (~3%/month on average balance) | ~-110,000 ₮               |
| Non-payment loss 5%                            | ~-90,000 ₮                |
| **Left**                                       | **~400,000 ₮ (~13%)**     |

At 15% non-payment profit roughly halves (~200,000 ₮). Keeping default risk low is the main lever.

With Korean customers, if installments run through Korean card 무이자 할부 via a PG, the card issuer carries the credit risk and we receive the full amount (minus PG fee), so the cost-of-capital and default lines mostly disappear. That is the recommended v1 setup.

## Why inbound is lower risk than outbound

- Koreans enter Mongolia visa-free (trial since 2024; **verify still valid in 2026**).
- No overstay/non-return risk like Mongolians going to Korea.
- Korean credit system and payment discipline are strong.
